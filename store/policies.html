Shipping

Free shipping on all US orders. Orders are processed in 1–3 business days and usually delivered in 5–9 business days. Tracking is emailed when your order ships. Some items ship separately from different warehouses.

Returns & refunds

Contact us within 30 days of delivery to start a return. Items must be unused and in original packaging. If an item arrives damaged or wrong, send a photo and we will replace or refund it at no cost to you. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Privacy

We collect your name, email, shipping address and order details only to fulfill your order and provide support. Payments are handled by our payment processor; we never see or store your card number. This site uses advertising pixels (Meta, TikTok) to measure ad performance. We do not sell your personal information.
