# Mildwell business plan (one page)

**Positioning:** "Know your water. Protect your skin." Shower and bath water information and products for people with eczema and sensitive skin, from a founder who has eczema.

## What the research says (and what we may claim)
- Several observational studies link **hard water** to a higher chance of eczema in children (UK, Danish cohorts). Hard water leaves more soap residue on skin.
- Water softener trials have **not** shown softening treats existing eczema.
- **Chlorine and chloramine** can dry and irritate skin, and many people with eczema report relief from filtering them.
- **Shower filters reduce chlorine but don't soften water.** Chloramine needs vitamin C or catalytic-carbon media.
- **Claims:** water facts with sources, and "reduces chlorine/chloramine" backed by lab data. Never "treats, cures or prevents eczema."

Sources: SOFTER trial protocol (PMC6707708), National Eczema Association on hard water, shower filter market about $0.7–1B growing about 9% a year.

**Watch-outs:** health claims (FTC), supplier quality (insist on independent test reports), competitors (Second Shower already targets eczema, so win on the free tool, honesty and your story).
