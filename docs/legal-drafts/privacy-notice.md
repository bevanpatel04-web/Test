# Privacy notice: employer rewards program

*Draft of 2026-11-14. Items in [brackets] are placeholders to decide with the attorney.*

This notice explains what **Patient Money** (working name), "we," collects when you join a rewards program your employer offers. It also covers why we collect it, who sees it, how long we keep it, and your choices.

**The short version:**
- We collect only what we need to run the program.
- **We don't sell your personal information.**
- We don't use it for targeted ads.

## What we collect, and from whom

**From your employer:**
- your employee ID number;
- your hire date and, if you leave, your last day and whether you left by choice;
- your work location;
- a yes/no flag that you are eligible (for example, 18 or older and in a participating role);
- if your employer sends your invite through us, your work email or mobile number.

We do **not** get your Social Security number, date of birth, pay rate, bank details, performance reviews or medical information.

- a display name;
- the email address you want gift cards sent to;
- an optional mobile number for texts;

## Why we use it

- To run the program: track points, process redemptions, send gift cards and make payroll bonus files for your employer.
- To send program messages: milestone reminders and redemption confirmations. Texts only go out if you agree; reply STOP to end them.
- To give your employer reports.
  - Totals, such as how many people joined and how retention compares.
  - Per member: points, redemptions and lessons completed, shown under your display name and employee ID.
- To prevent fraud and keep the program secure.
- To improve lessons and the app, using totals that don't identify you.

**What your employer does not see:** your lesson or checklist answers, or any savings account details.

- **Your employer**, as described above.
- **Service providers** that help us run the program, under contracts that limit their use of your data:

  - our payroll data connection;

- **A buyer**, if our company is sold or merged. They must keep the same promises.

**We do not sell or rent your personal information, or share it for targeted advertising.**

## Age

The program is only for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

## Security

- We encrypt data in transit and at rest.
- Only staff who need access have it.
- We review our providers' security.
- No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and your employer as the law requires.
