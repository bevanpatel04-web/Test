# Employer pilot agreement: outline

*Draft of 2026-11-14 by finance and ops. Items in [brackets] are placeholders to decide with the attorney and the employer. This is an outline of terms, not a finished contract.*

## 1. Parties and purpose

- **Patient Money** (working name; legal entity to be formed) ("we") and **[Employer legal name]** ("Employer").
- Purpose: a 90-day test of an employee rewards and money-education program for Employer's hourly staff. It measures enrollment, engagement, cost and retention.

## 2. Scope

**We provide:**
- the member app and lessons;
- points tracking under the agreed earning rules (Schedule A);
- an employer dashboard;
- payroll bonus files;
- gift card fulfillment;
- member support;
- day-30, day-60 and day-90 reports.

**Employer provides:**
- a named program owner;
- the minimum data in Section 6, sent on time;
- help promoting enrollment (posters, onboarding mention);
- payroll processing of earned payroll bonuses.

**Not in the pilot:**
- any investment product, securities or brokerage account;
- any movement of money by us into members' accounts;
- any rewards for trading, number of trades or account balances.

**Lessons are general education.** They are not investment, tax or legal advice.

## 3. Eligibility [attorney: key issue]

- Draft: hourly employees aged **18 and over** at the participating locations [list].
- **Open question.** Our product is designed for 18-25 year olds. But the federal Age Discrimination in Employment Act protects workers aged 40 and over in "pay" and "benefits" (EEOC, https://www.eeoc.gov/age-discrimination), and some states also protect younger workers.
  - Offering a pay-like reward only to ages 18-25 may create risk.
  - Default unless counsel advises otherwise: open the program to all hourly staff aged 18+, and aim the content and marketing at young workers.
- Joining is voluntary. Joining or not joining does not affect anyone's job, schedule or pay.

## 4. Term

- **90 days** from the launch date [date].
- It can be extended once by [30] days in writing.
- At day 90, Employer chooses to (a) convert to the paid plan in Section 5 or (b) end the pilot under Section 11.

## 5. Fees

- **Pilot fee: [$1,500] flat**, invoiced at signing, due within [15] days. [Pilot 1 design partner: $0 to $500.]
- **If Employer converts:** the pilot fee is credited against the first 3 paid months.
- **Paid plan (for reference):**
  - [$4] per enrolled employee per month, with a [$500] monthly minimum;
  - "enrolled" means enrolled on the 1st of each month;
  - [12]-month term, billed monthly by ACH;
  - price locked for [12] months.
- The fees do not include the reward pool (Section 6A).

## 6A. Reward pool: funding and refunds

- **Budget.** Employer sets a reward budget for the pilot: [$1,000 per 150 enrolled employees]. We alert Employer when [80%] is used.
- **Points earned are honored.** Points earned under Schedule A before the pilot ends are honored even if the budget runs out. Employer agrees to fund them. [Attorney: earned points may be treated as wages.]
- **Payroll bonus.** Employer pays it through its own payroll, in the next regular paycheck after redemption. Employer handles withholding, payroll taxes and W-2 reporting. We send a redemption file each pay period showing employee ID, amount and date.
- **Gift cards.** [Option 1: Employer funds a gift card account in its own name, which we operate under its authority.] [Option 2: we buy gift cards from our vendor and invoice Employer at cost, with no markup, monthly in arrears.]
  - Either way, **we do not hold Employer's money in advance.** [Attorney: confirm this avoids money transmitter and prepaid access rules.]
  - Gift cards given to employees are taxable wages (IRS Pub 15-B). We report each gift card redemption to Employer for payroll reporting.
- **Refunds.** No prepaid pool means nothing to refund. If any amount is prepaid, the unused part is refunded within [30] days after the pilot ends. We first keep enough to cover points members have earned and can still redeem (Section 11).
- **No markup.** We do not take a fee or a share of the reward pool.

## 6B. Data: what is shared, and the minimum we need

| Field | Why we need it | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Employee ID (Employer's internal number, or one we assign) | Match points and payroll files without using names | Yes |
| Hire date | Tenure milestones | Yes |
| End date and whether the departure was voluntary | Retention measurement; ending points | Yes |
| Location | Location reports; staggered-start comparison | Yes |
| Eligibility flag (18+, hourly, participating location) | Confirms who can join, without collecting date of birth | Yes |
| Work email or mobile number, **only if** Employer sends invites through us | Sending the invite | Optional; Employer can hand out invite codes instead |
| Same hire and end dates for eligible staff who did **not** join, with IDs only | Comparison group for retention | Yes, for the retention report |

**We will not ask for:**
- Social Security numbers or dates of birth;
- pay rates or bank details;
- performance, discipline or medical information;
- immigration status.

**Members give us directly:** a display name, an email for gift cards, an optional phone number for texts (with consent), lesson progress and checklist completion.

**Employer sees:**
- aggregate reports;
- each member's points, redemptions and lessons completed, shown under a display name;
- payroll redemption files by employee ID.

Employer does **not** see lesson answers, checklist answers or savings details.

**Data use limits:**
- **No sale of data.** We do not sell or rent personal data and do not use it for targeted ads.
- We use Employer data only to run the program and report to Employer.
- Any benchmark across employers is aggregated and de-identified, and only with Employer's written consent [opt-in].

**Security, breaches and deletion:**
- Security: encryption in transit and at rest, access limited to staff who need it, [security standard].
- Breaches: we notify Employer within [72] hours of confirming a breach affecting its data.
- At the end: we delete or return Employer data within [60] days. We keep only what law requires, such as tax-related redemption records for [X years].

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential, uses it only for the pilot, and protects it with reasonable care. These obligations last [2] years after the pilot ends. Trade secrets and personal data are protected for as long as the law allows.

## 8. Employer responsibilities

- Pay wages, bonuses and payroll taxes, and file W-2s.
- Follow wage-and-hour and anti-discrimination laws.
- Keep participation voluntary and never retaliate against anyone for joining or not joining.
- Tell employees the program exists and that joining is optional.

## 9. Our responsibilities

- Run the program as described.
- Keep earning rules free of trading or balance-based rewards.
- Answer member support requests within [2] business days.
- Deliver gift cards within [5] business days of redemption.
- Provide the day-30, day-60 and day-90 reports.

## 10. Success measures (agreed at signing)

- Enrollment: [50%] or more of eligible staff.
- 30-day active members: [50%] or more.
- 90-day new-hire retention, members vs comparison group (staggered start or the same period last year).
- Employer manager time: under [2] hours a month.
- Pool spend vs budget.

These measures are for the conversion decision. They are not a promise of any result.

## 11. Termination

- Either party may end the pilot with [14] days' written notice, or immediately for a material breach not fixed within [10] days.
- **Points.** Points members earned before the end date stay redeemable for [90] days, and Employer funds them. [Attorney: state final-pay rules may require payout at separation; see member terms.]
- **Fees.** The pilot fee is [non-refundable / refundable pro rata if we end the pilot without cause].
- **Data** is handled under Section 6B.

## Questions for the attorney

1. **Age eligibility.** Can rewards be limited to ages 18-25 under the ADEA and state law? If not, is "18+ with content for young workers" fine?
2. **Wage law.** Are earned points "wages"? Can unredeemed points expire after someone leaves, or must they be paid in the final paycheck? Does the answer differ by state?
3. **Gift card and money handling.** Which structure (Section 6A, Option 1 or 2) keeps us outside money transmitter and prepaid access licensing?
4. **Tax reporting.** What is the cleanest way for Employer to report gift card redemptions as wages, and should Employer gross them up?
5. **Text messages.** What consent do we need for program texts?
6. **Employee data privacy.** Which state privacy laws apply to us as Employer's service provider (for example, California's rules for employee data)? Do we need a separate data processing addendum?
7. **Securities.** Confirm the pilot (education plus non-securities rewards) raises no securities issues, and list what must change before points can become investing deposits through a licensed partner.
