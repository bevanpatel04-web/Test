# Rewards program terms (members)

*Draft of 2026-11-14. Items in [brackets] are placeholders to decide with the attorney and each employer.*

## The basics

- This program is run by **Patient Money** (working name), "we." Your employer pays for it.
- You earn points for staying at your job, finishing money lessons and building healthy money habits.
- **100 points = $1.**
- Joining is your choice. Joining or not joining does not affect your job, schedule or pay.

## Who can join

- You must be **18 or older**.
- You must work for a participating employer at a participating location [in an eligible role].
- [Attorney: confirm whether eligibility can be limited by age band.]
- One account per person.

## How you earn points

| What you do | Points |
|---|---|
| 30 days on the job | 300 |
| 90 days on the job | 300 |
| 180 days on the job | 600 |
| One year on the job | 1,000 |
| Finish a lesson | 50 each, up to 10 lessons |
| Finish the "before you invest" checklist | 200, once |
| Set up direct deposit to a savings account | 300, once |

- Job milestones count from your hire date.
- You earn a milestone only if you reach it **after you join** the program. [Confirm with each employer.]
- The most you can earn in your first year is 3,200 points ($32).

**What never earns points:**
- buying or selling investments;
- the number of trades you make;
- how much money is in any account.

We don't reward any of these.

## Using your points

- You can redeem once you have **500 points**.
- **Payroll bonus:** your employer pays it in your next paycheck. It is taxed like regular pay.
- **Gift card:** delivered by email within 5 business days.
- Points have **no cash value** except through these redemption options.
- You can't sell, trade or transfer points, and they can't be combined with another person's account.

## Taxes

- Rewards are taxable pay.
- **Payroll bonuses** are wages in your paycheck, so tax is withheld like regular pay.
- **Gift cards** are also taxable wages under IRS rules: gift cards are never treated as a small, tax-free perk (IRS Publication 15-B). Your employer adds their value to your wages and may withhold tax. [Employer to confirm method.]
- We don't give tax advice. For your own situation, see the IRS website or a tax professional.

## If you leave your job

- You keep the points you earned while you worked there.
- You have **[90] days** after your last day to redeem them.
- [Attorney: some states may treat earned rewards as wages that must be paid when you leave. Choose by state: (a) automatic payout in your final paycheck, or (b) points expire after the redemption window.]
- You stop earning new points on your last day.

## If your employer leaves the program, or the program ends

- We will tell you at least **[30] days** before.
- Points you have already earned stay redeemable for [90] days after the end date.

## Changes to these terms

- We may change these terms or the earning rules.
- If a change reduces the value of points you already have, or makes them harder to use, we will tell you at least **[30] days** before it takes effect.
- We will not take away points you have already earned, except for fraud or mistakes (below).

## Fraud and mistakes

- We may remove points that came from fraud, a technical error, or breaking these terms. Examples: fake accounts, sharing a login, or automating lessons.
- In serious cases we may close the account.
- If we remove points by mistake, we will put them back.
- If you think we got it wrong, contact us (below). A person will review it within [10] business days.

## Lessons are education, not advice

- Lessons are general education for everyone. They are not advice about what you should do with your own money.
- We don't recommend any investment, fund, coin, broker or app.
- Any example about growth is a labeled hypothetical, not a prediction. All investments can lose value, including the money you put in.
- This program has no investment products. We never move your money.

## Your privacy

- Our privacy notice explains what we collect and why.
- In short: we don't sell your data.
- Your employer sees your points, your redemptions and how many lessons you finished. Your employer does not see your lesson or checklist answers.
