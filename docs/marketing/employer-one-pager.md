# Keep your first-year hourly staff longer: a 90-day pilot

**DRAFT for attorney review.** Pricing and terms below are DRAFT assumptions, not an offer. Patient Money is a working name. Contact: pilot@example.com

*For HR and operations leaders at restaurant groups, hotels and retailers. Last updated 2026-11-14.*

## The problem: early-career staff leave fast, and replacing them costs money

- **Quits are high in your industry.** Accommodation and food services had **719,000 quits in September 2026, a 5.0% monthly quits rate**, against 2.1% for all nonfarm jobs. Retail trade had 475,000 quits (3.1%). ([BLS JOLTS, released Nov 3, 2026](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/jolts_11032026.htm)). One month can be noisy: the accommodation and food services rate was 3.5% in August 2026 ([BLS, Oct 13, 2026](https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2026/quits-rate-3-5-percent-in-accommodation-and-food-services-august-2026.htm)).
- **Your workforce is young.** 21.5% of people working in food services and drinking places are 20 to 24, compared with 8.8% of all workers. In clothing retail it's 22.1%. (Our math from [BLS CPS Table 18b, 2025 annual averages](https://www.bls.gov/cps/cpsaat18b.htm). BLS groups ages as 16-19 and 20-24, so there is no exact 18-25 figure.)
- **Young workers have short tenure.** The median worker aged 20 to 24 has been with their employer 1.5 years. Leisure and hospitality has the lowest median tenure of any industry, 2.4 years ([BLS Employee Tenure, January 2026 data, released Sept 24, 2026](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/tenure.nr0.htm)).
- **Each exit costs you.** A review of published case studies put the typical cost of turnover for jobs paying under $30,000 a year at **16% of annual pay** ([Center for American Progress, Nov 2012](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/there-are-significant-business-costs-to-replacing-employees/)). That's an older, broad estimate. Your own number is better.
- **Money stress is common.** 59% of employees say they're stressed about their finances ([PwC 2026 Employee Financial Wellness Survey](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/human-resources/library/employee-financial-wellness-survey.html), online survey of about 3,500 US employees, January 2026; a private survey, not government data).

## The offer: a 90-day pilot

At least 4 locations and 250 or more eligible hourly staff, 90 days. We start the program at about half your locations; the rest start after day 90 and are the comparison group until then. Your staff earn points for staying, for finishing two-minute money lessons, and for two healthy money habits. Points turn into a payroll bonus or a gift card that you fund. At day 90 you get a retention report and decide whether to continue, change or stop.

## How it works

1. **Set up (week 0, about 2 hours of your time).** Pick the locations and fund the reward pool. Send a roster file: employee ID, hire date, location, and active or end date. No Social Security numbers, bank details or pay rates. We send a QR-code poster and a 60-second pre-shift huddle script.
2. **Staff join and earn (days 1-90).** Joining is optional and takes a phone. Pilot default points, at 100 points = $1:

   | What the employee does | Points |
   |---|---|
   | Reaches 30 days on the job | 300 |
   | Reaches 90 days | 300 |
   | Reaches 180 days | 600 |
   | Reaches one year | 1,000 |
   | Finishes a two-minute lesson | 50 each, up to 10 |
   | Finishes the "before you invest" checklist | 200, once |
   | Sets up direct deposit to a savings account | 300, once |

   Points are **never** given for trading, number of trades or account balances.
3. **Pay out and review (monthly, then day 90).** Members redeem 500 points or more as a payroll bonus (you pay it in your next payroll run, from a file we send) or a gift card. At day 90 we walk you through the retention report.

## What you get

- **A live dashboard:** eligible, enrolled and active staff, points issued and redeemed, reward pool remaining. Staff appear by display name only.
- **A monthly redemption file** for your payroll team.
- **A day-90 retention report:** month by month, the share of staff still employed at your program locations next to your waiting locations. We also show enrolled vs. not enrolled, with its limits spelled out.

**Please read this part.** The report is not a controlled experiment. People who choose to join may already be more likely to stay. So a gap between the two groups can't prove the pilot caused it. To make the comparison fairer, we can also show your own retention for the same season last year, or start some locations a month later than others.

Rewards are front-loaded to the first 90 days, when new hires are most likely to leave: $30 at 30 days, $40 at 90 days, $30 at 6 months and $30 at one year, plus up to $20 for lessons and two money habits. If one replacement costs you about $4,000 (16% of a $25,000 yearly wage), the pilot costs about as much as 1.5 replacements. The report can't prove the pilot kept anyone. This is only a way to size the bet.

## What it is not

- **Not investment advice.** Lessons are general education. We don't recommend any security, fund, coin, broker or app, and we don't predict returns.
- **No securities and no money movement by us in the pilot.** No investing accounts are opened. Payroll bonuses go through your payroll.
- **Rewards are taxable pay.** Rewards paid through payroll are taxed as wages. Gift cards from an employer are also taxable wages: IRS rules say cash and gift cards "no matter how little, are never excludable" as a small fringe benefit ([IRS Publication 15-B, 2026](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15b)).
- **Not a rewards-for-trading program.** In January 2024, Massachusetts securities regulators fined a trading app $7.5 million, partly over game-like features such as digital confetti and "scratch-off" tickets ([consent order](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/securities/download/RH-Consent-Order.pdf)). We reward tenure, learning and saving habits only.
- **Not a fix for pay, schedules or managers.** If those drive your exits, points won't solve it.

## FAQ

**Who can join?** Hourly staff aged 18 or older at the pilot locations. The lessons are written for people early in their working lives, but we suggest you don't limit eligibility by age. Federal law protects workers 40 and older from age discrimination, including in benefits, and some states protect younger workers too ([EEOC](https://www.eeoc.gov/age-discrimination), checked Nov 14, 2026). Your counsel should confirm.

**Do milestone rewards affect overtime pay?** Possibly. Bonuses promised in advance, like attendance bonuses, are usually "nondiscretionary" and count toward the regular rate used for overtime ([DOL Fact Sheet #56C, Dec 2019](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/56c-bonuses)). Tenure rewards likely work the same way. Check with your payroll provider.

**What do employees see?** A simple page with their points, history and next milestone. No ads and no product pitches.

**Do you sell anything to my staff?** No. During the pilot there is nothing to buy and no partner offers.

**What happens to employee data?** We use the roster only to run the pilot and the report. We don't sell it. A full data agreement comes with the DRAFT pilot terms.

**What do my managers have to do?** Hang the poster, read the huddle script once, and send the roster file each week.

**What happens later?** After the pilot, we plan to let members send points as cash to their *own* savings or investing account through a licensed partner. Members would choose what to do with it, and we would never pick investments. That needs its own legal review and is not part of this pilot.
