## Before you commit (about 30 minutes, free)

1. **Trademark:** search the name at the USPTO trademark search (tmsearch.uspto.gov), in classes 11 (water filters) and 3 (skincare).
2. **Domain:** check `mildwell.com` / `getmildwell.com` / `mildwell.co` at any registrar (Namecheap, Cloudflare, Porkbun). About $10–15 a year.
3. **Social handles:** check Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest for `@mildwell` (or `@mildwellwater`).

## Competitors to know

- **Second Shower** already sells shower filters for eczema and markets chloramine removal. That confirms demand. Stand out with the free ZIP water tool, honest science, and your own eczema story.
- **Jolie, Canopy, AquaBliss, Filterbaby:** general "beauty" shower filters. They target hair and skin broadly, not eczema.
