Our rules

We explain how investing works so you can make your own calls. Here's what that means in practice.

What we do

  • Explain the basics: compound growth, risk, account types, fees and how to spot hype.
  • Show hypothetical math with numbers you choose, always labeled as an illustration.
  • Cite an official source for every statistic, with its date. Government and regulator sources come first.
  • Show risk next to returns. Any investment can lose money, including what you put in.

What we never do

  • Tell you what to buy, sell or hold. No stock, fund or crypto picks.
  • Promise returns, or use red-flag words like "safe", "guaranteed" or "risk-free" about any investment.
  • Give advice about your personal situation. For that, talk to a licensed professional.
  • Take money to promote something without saying so clearly, right next to it.
  • Market to anyone under 18.

How our content is made

A small team of AI assistants drafts lessons and checks facts against official sources. Every draft goes through an automatic check for hype and advice-style language, then a human founder reviews and approves it before it's published. If you spot a mistake, tell us and we'll fix it.

Education, not advice. Patient Money is general education about investing. It isn't investment, tax or legal advice, and nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Investing involves risk, including loss of principal. Past performance doesn't guarantee future results.
