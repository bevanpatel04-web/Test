How it works

  1. Set up (week 0, about 2 hours of your time). Pick 1 to 3 locations and fund the reward pool. Send a roster file with employee ID, hire date, location, and active or end date. No Social Security numbers, bank details or pay rates. We send a QR-code poster and a 60-second huddle script.
  2. Staff join and earn (days 1-90). Joining is optional and works on a phone. Points come from work milestones, lessons and two money habits. Never from trading, number of trades or account balances.
  3. Pay out and review. Members redeem 500 points or more as a payroll bonus, which you pay from a monthly file we send, or as a gift card. At day 90 we walk you through the retention report.

An honest note on the report. It isn't a controlled experiment. People who choose to join may already be more likely to stay, so a gap between the groups can't prove the pilot caused it. Pilot terms

DRAFT for attorney review · not an offer

  • Length and size: 90 days, 1 to 3 locations, 50 to 500 eligible hourly staff aged 18 or older.
  • Platform fee: $4 per enrolled employee per month. You pay only for staff who join.
  • Reward pool: you fund it. At the default rate, one member can earn at most about $20 during a 90-day pilot. You can choose a richer points rate.
  • Taxes: payroll bonuses are taxed as wages, and your usual employer payroll taxes apply. Gift cards from an employer are taxable wages too (IRS Publication 15-B, 2026).
  • Draft assumptions to confirm: no setup fee, no automatic renewal, and either side can end the pilot with 14 days' notice.

Example (illustration, not a quote): 200 enrolled staff for 3 months is $2,400 in platform fees plus up to $4,000 in rewards, so up to $6,400 before payroll taxes. If one replacement costs you about $4,000 (16% of a $25,000 yearly wage), the pilot costs about as much as 1.6 replacements. The report can't prove the pilot kept anyone; this only helps you size the bet.

What this pilot is not

  • Not investment advice. Lessons are general education. We don't recommend any security, fund, coin, broker or app, and we don't predict returns.
  • No securities and no money movement by us. No investing accounts are opened. Payroll bonuses go through your payroll.
  • Not a rewards-for-trading program. Points come only from tenure, lessons and saving habits.
  • Not a fix for pay, schedules or managers. If those drive your exits, points won't solve it.

Questions employers ask

Who can join?

Hourly staff aged 18 or older at the pilot locations. The lessons are written for people early in their working lives, but we suggest you don't limit eligibility by age. Federal law protects workers 40 and older from age discrimination, including in benefits, and some states protect younger workers too (EEOC). Your counsel should confirm.

Do milestone rewards affect overtime pay?

Possibly. Bonuses promised in advance, such as attendance bonuses, are usually "nondiscretionary" and count toward the regular rate used for overtime (DOL Fact Sheet #56C, Dec 2019). Tenure rewards likely work the same way. Check with your payroll provider.

Do you sell anything to my staff?

No. During the pilot there is nothing to buy, no ads and no partner offers.

What happens to employee data?

We use the roster only to run the pilot and the report, and we don't sell it. Your dashboard shows display names, not personal details. A full data agreement comes with the DRAFT pilot terms.

What do my managers have to do?

Hang the poster, read the huddle script once, and send the roster file each week.

Why don't points reward trading?

Game-like features that push trading are a regulatory red flag. In January 2024, Massachusetts securities regulators fined a trading app $7.5 million, partly over features such as digital confetti and "scratch-off" tickets (consent order). We reward tenure, learning and saving habits only.

What happens after the pilot?

You decide whether to continue, change or stop. Later, we plan to let members send points as cash to their own savings or investing account through a licensed partner, with members choosing what to do with it. We would never pick investments. That needs its own legal review and is not part of this pilot.
