How our videos are made

Our video lessons are made with AI: narrated motion graphics with real charts. Some use an AI voice and some are captions only. There are no fake human presenters.

  • AI drafts each script from the lesson plan.
  • Facts come only from official sources, with their dates.
  • An automatic check flags hype, predictions and advice-style language.
  • A person reviews and approves every video before members see it.

Every video is labeled "Made with AI, reviewed by a person." The review checks accuracy, sources and tone. It isn't personal advice. If you spot a mistake, email hello@example.com and we'll fix it and say so.
